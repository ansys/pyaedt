Add explicit cable harness modeling in HFSS through the new
``ansys.aedt.core.modeler.advanced_cad.cable_harness`` subpackage. ``RoutedCableBundle`` builds a
fully explicit three-dimensional model of a routed, twisted, shielded cable bundle from a
configuration file: it creates the materials, the twisted conductors, the insulation, the pair and
overall shields and the jacket, assigns frequency-dependent transfer-impedance boundary conditions
to every shield sheet, creates the ports and the differential pairs, and creates the broadband
S-parameter setup. ``read_configuration_file`` now also accepts YAML files.
